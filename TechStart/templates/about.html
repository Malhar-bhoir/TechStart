About TechStart

Empowering beginners to master computer skills and programming through personalized, AI-driven education.

Our Mission

TechStart was created to bridge the digital divide. Learning computer basics or a new programming language can be intimidating. Traditional tutorials are static and can't answer your specific doubts. TechStart solves this by providing an interactive, patient, and highly knowledgeable AI Tutor that guides you step-by-step, in your preferred language.

Custom Fine-Tuned AI

Unlike standard chatbots, TechStart is powered by a custom-trained Small Language Model (SLM) based on Qwen 2.5. It has been specifically fine-tuned on thousands of educational Q&A pairs to act as a perfect, patient tutor.

Multilingual Support

Education should not be limited by language barriers. Our AI seamlessly understands and teaches in English, Hindi, and Marathi, making technology accessible to a broader audience across India.
